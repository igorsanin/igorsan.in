For 17 years I've been making films that explain complex things — energy, finance, cybersecurity, cities — and making them worth watching.

I come from screenwriting and classical directing. In 2011 I founded my first video studio; later, as Creative & Managing Director of Mercator, I turned a long-established infographics studio into a creative hub known for humour and unexpected formats. Since 2021 I've worked independently with my own hybrid production team — CG artists, AI specialists, editors and a camera crew in Valencia — and as writer and director for partner studios.

My work has run on the facade of the Burj Khalifa, on immersive LED installations and on the opening screens of international business forums. It has earned two Silver Dolphins at Cannes Corporate, a Silver World Medal at New York Festivals, and awards at Metal Vision and AKMR.

Brands I've worked on include Komatsu, Hyundai, AstraZeneca, KFC, Kaspersky, Group-IB, Play-Doh and Coral Travel.

## How I can help

Creative direction, scripts and direction for brand, corporate and exhibition films. Turnkey hybrid production with my team. AI pipelines and training for in-house teams.

Based in Valencia, Spain. Working worldwide in English and Russian.
