A story of courage, perseverance and faith. It follows the expedition led by Colonel Nikolai Voloshinov, sent to Siberia to assess whether the Baikal–Amur railway could be built. In his report he concluded it was impossible.

Later generations proved him wrong: advances in science and technology made it possible to cut through mountains, drain swamps and lay track across the vast Siberian expanse. The film is dedicated to the builders of the railway.
