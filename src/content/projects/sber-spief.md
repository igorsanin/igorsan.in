At big forums companies fight for attention: screens get bigger and content louder. Sometimes visitors just want to stop and breathe.

So for the 2025 forum we made calm, slow screensavers of recognisable natural landscapes — mountains, a frozen lake, taiga forests and the northern lights. The images were generated with several neural networks, but we made sure they kept the real character of each place. Then we went further and imagined the brand as part of nature, with the logo built into the landscapes.
