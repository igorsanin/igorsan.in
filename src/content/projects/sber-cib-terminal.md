An image film for the Sber CIB Terminal trading and analytics platform. The goal was to show the interface as clearly and engagingly as possible, so potential clients see its value right away.

The concept brings trading tools into the physical world. The order book becomes a glass tumbling off a table — market instability. A sundial tips over — lost time. Money turns into cubes that demand skilful control. Together these metaphors build one story: the terminal brings order to market chaos.

The second half reveals the interface itself: key modules and windows shown through realistic textures, macro shots, glass and dramatic light. Everything was made in CG.
