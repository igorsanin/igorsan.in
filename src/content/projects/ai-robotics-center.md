For the presentation of Sber's first anthropomorphic robot we were asked to create a high-tech opening sequence of detailed, close-up hardware shots. The main film followed a traditional production path; for the opening we went hybrid.

Rough phone footage and photos taken in the robotics lab became structural blueprints, and AI tools generated the final high-fidelity visuals. The design stayed exact, and there was no need for a specialised macro studio shoot.

The whole sequence was made by one specialist in two days. Beyond saving time and budget, the workflow made it possible to change lighting, textures and camera angles on the fly without re-shooting.
