Projecting a brand narrative onto the world's tallest structure calls for a different kind of composition. The challenge in this facade projection for Kaspersky in Dubai was fitting complex technology themes onto an extreme, tapering vertical canvas.

Instead of forcing widescreen layouts onto the tower, we made the building an active participant in the story. Its tiers and contours became data environments: the silhouette morphs into a minimal industrial complex, fills like a vessel with thousands of red threat pyramids, and is textured with living neural-network patterns.

Tied to the architectural relief, the 60-second mapping turned the landmark into a monument to digital resilience and cyber immunity.
