In 2024 the Runet Prize was held for the 21st time, in a hall of the Tretyakov Gallery — and the venue shaped the whole concept.

For 15 nominations we created original paintings in the style of different Russian artists, each depicting a key moment in the history of the internet industry: bears in a forest discovering a new web portal, Anna Akhmatova browsing a site for poets, the opening of an internet café through the eyes of Petrov-Vodkin.

The images were generated with a neural network and then finished with hand-drawn animation of painterly brushwork, to match the status of the evening.
