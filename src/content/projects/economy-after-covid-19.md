An opening film for the 2021 financial forum, on the impact of COVID-19 on the world economy. The old, fragile approaches to managing the economy are falling apart; the new reality needs structural change. The film sets out four priorities: fair social support, faster digitalisation, the green transition and a shift from "people for the economy" to "an economy for people".
