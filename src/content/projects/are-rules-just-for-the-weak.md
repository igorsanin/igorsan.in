An animated film about animals who ignore the three main rules of equipment safety. The aim was to present safety requirements in a new and unusual way that would make staff stop and think about how they follow the rules.

Nature gave chameleons, monkeys, hedgehogs and tortoises ways to protect themselves. We wondered: what if the coolest of them simply refused to use them? They find excuses, just like people do: "rules aren't for cool people", "a little won't hurt", "it's not working hours". The consequences are tragic.
