While traditional retail is going through an apocalypse, e-commerce grows by more than 20% a year. But online isn't the enemy of offline: in the New Retail model they merge to give customers more choice, faster delivery and better service.

The opener for the New Retail forum showed how the industry is changing and what that means for its players. Just as a meteorite wiped out the dinosaurs, New Retail will clear the market of everyone who isn't ready to change. We told it as a futurological comedy.
