Together with the Day N team we created the video content for the city's stand at a major exhibition of transport innovations.

The screen itself was unusual: a ring we affectionately called the donut. Across its three surfaces rotated a collage of micro-presentations of the city's products and innovations — more than 30 of them. For each one we came up with an original idea and brought it to life in naked-eye 3D.
