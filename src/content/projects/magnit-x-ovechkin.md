In spring 2025 hockey star Alexander Ovechkin was close to the all-time goal-scoring record. Just before the moment, the retail chain Magnit launched a campaign with him as the face of the brand: in the commercial he smashes through a thousand pucks and charges on toward new goals.

Ovechkin couldn't be at the shoot, so the team brought the idea to life with deepfake technology.
