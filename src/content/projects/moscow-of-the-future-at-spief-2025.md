At an international economic forum, the city government wanted to give guests an interactive tour of its largest development projects — not as static presentations but as an immersive experience. The developer built a custom 320-degree panoramic space controlled from a touch interface. We produced three cinematic films and the application that ran them.

**The new Olimpiyskiy.** A stadium built for the 1980 Olympics reopens after a full transformation. The film lets visitors see it today: a dive from the high board, laps on a track with city views, a concert arena and a revolving restaurant.

**Bolshoy City.** A business district becomes a neighbourhood that combines towers with comfortable housing, quiet green courtyards and public spaces.

**Mnyovniki floodplain.** A once-neglected area turns into a community with new housing, sports grounds, its own beach and a world-famous circus — shown through the eyes of a young local guide.

On the touchscreen visitors pick an area, a site and a mode of visit; the matching film plays on the panorama and the physical model lights up.
