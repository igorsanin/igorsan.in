In 2022 we made the full identity of the Runet Prize ceremony with generative AI — one of the first projects of its kind.

The Prize celebrates the most influential web projects and initiatives of the year, and AI was the trend that shaped visual culture that year. So we used it: a team of fourteen artists and designers worked with Midjourney and Stable Diffusion, building openers, nomination screens and stage graphics around the idea of human and machine intelligence working side by side.
