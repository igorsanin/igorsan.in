A full-CG film with 3D infographics showing what happens inside a battery during charging, discharging and energy recovery — on the examples of electric buses, river transport and electric cars.

In the electric-transport zone of a city technology exhibition, the centrepiece was a large battery-shaped screen. We made the film specifically for it, synchronised with the installation's LED lighting for a fully immersive effect. The installation drew hundreds of visitors a day.
