Sber supports students and graduate students through grants, educational programmes and career events. This was the subject of two films we created entirely with generative AI tools.

We built a full cast of virtual animated actors in one unified style, matched to the real parameters of their prototypes. Every location was created separately and used as an asset, which kept the architecture and style consistent from scene to scene.
