The company's offices have dozens of screens showing content to employees and guests every day — and people had stopped noticing them. To beat banner blindness, the company launched a series of living pictures made with AI.

Three collections changed with the seasons. **Future Renaissance** combined today's technology with the classical style of Renaissance painters: neural networks generated the images, an illustrator refined them, and animators brought each one to life by hand, with the company's real products hidden in every picture. **Other Worlds** imagined the working day outside the office — underwater, in space, in fairy tales. The third collection played with the logo in different places and styles; it became the official desktop wallpaper and the start screen of the mobile app.

The office screens differ in proportions, so each piece was made in 14 versions — 504 screensavers in total.
