Transport systems change the shape of a city and set it in motion. We showed that by letting routes from metro maps, suburban rail and other transport lines burst into 3D space.

Everything secondary stays minimal, while the route lines and key landmarks — the actors of the film — are vividly coloured and textured for realism.
