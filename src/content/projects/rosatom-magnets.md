Global demand for rare-earth magnets is expected to triple by 2030. We made a visual presentation of a new large-scale production facility designed to process raw materials into finished magnets.

Advanced 3D visualisation explains why rare-earth metals matter, follows the path from raw material to finished product, and shows the plant's planned capacity of 1,000 tonnes a year by 2028.
