In 2018 a cooking TV channel launched a rebrand, and we built a new design inspired by folklore and fairy tales. Every ID became a witty story starring ingredients: a cabbage as the riddle of "a hundred layers of clothes and not one button", a pumpkin, a pepper, rejuvenating apples, the giant turnip, axe porridge and corn as the Snow Maiden.
