At a city exhibition about 2030, visitors stepped into an unusual attraction: an elevator whose walls were giant LED screens, turning the ride into a virtual journey through a new district.

Not long ago the area was an ageing industrial zone. Today it has towers, parks, thousands of trees, new schools and a whole new infrastructure — and our film told that transformation. We made it with Avilex, who built the real elevator: nine LED surfaces with a combined content resolution of more than 40K.

On a very tight schedule we built a virtual replica of the district, from streets and courtyards to apartment interiors, and filled it with animated residents to bring the city to life.
