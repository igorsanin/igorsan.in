What should a child do if they are bullied online? How do you spot an internet scam? Do hackers really give data back after a ransom?

On the internet, as in the forest, danger hides behind every bush — so it's better to go in prepared. This animated series follows Mole and Raccoon through ten episodes about digital threats and how to deal with them.
