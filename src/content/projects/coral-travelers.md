An animated series for Coral Travel built on reverse advice: the most reliable ways to ruin a holiday.

Pack the fishing rod, the samovar, your favourite ficus and a spare 19-litre water bottle. Throw your passport into the suitcase so finding it at check-in becomes an archaeological dig. Travel in your most formal clothes — it's a holiday, after all.

Each episode ends by turning the joke into a practical tip, all wrapped in the Instagram feed of a first-time traveller.
